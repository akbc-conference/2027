The 2027 edition of the Automated Knowledge Base Construction (AKBC) workshop will bring together researchers and practitioners working on the construction, integration, and use of structured knowledge.

Knowledge bases provide structured, reusable representations of facts and relationships. AKBC explores how to build and maintain these resources, and how they can support applications across natural language processing, knowledge representation, databases, and machine learning.

## About AKBC

AKBC has a long history of bringing together the knowledge base construction community. Previous editions include independent conferences in [2022](https://akbc.ws/2022), [2021](https://akbc.ws/2021), [2020](https://akbc.ws/2020), and [2019](https://www.akbc.ws/2019), as well as workshops at NIPS, NAACL, and CIKM.
